Part auto-theatre, part confessional, CARPARK is a solo performance staged inside a parked car. Created by Timothy George Kelly and performed by Yael Haskal, CARPARK invites four audience members at a time into a darkly funny, emotionally unfiltered journey through identity, disconnection, and longing in the age of the endless stream.
